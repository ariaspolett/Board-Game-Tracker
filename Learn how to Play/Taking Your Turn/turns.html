Taking Your Turn

Every turn has three steps, always in this order. First to 10 victory points on their own turn wins.

1. Roll for resources

Roll both dice. Every tile showing that number pays out to each player with a settlement (1 card) or city (2 cards) touching it.

Rolled a 7?

Nobody collects. Instead, move the robber to any tile. That tile stops producing while the robber sits on it. Players holding more than seven cards also have to discard half of them.

2. Trade

With players

Negotiate swaps with anyone. On your turn, others can only trade with you.

With the bank

Hand in four of one resource for one of your choice, or get a better rate through a harbor.

3. Build

Spend resources on roads, settlements, cities, or development cards, as many as you can afford.

Development cards

TypeCountWhat it does
Knight14Move the robber. Collecting 3 knights can earn Largest Army.
Progress6One-time bonus effects, then it's discarded.
Victory point5Worth 1 point. Keep it hidden until you win.

You can't play a card the same turn you bought it (victory point cards are the exception), and you usually play one development card per turn.

How points add up

SourcePoints
Settlement1 each
City2 each
Victory point card1 each
Longest Road (5+ connected roads)2
Largest Army (3+ knights played)2

Longest Road and Largest Army can change hands when someone beats the current holder.
