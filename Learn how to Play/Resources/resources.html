Resources

Everything you build in Catan is paid for with five resource types. Which ones you collect shapes your whole strategy.

Brick

Comes from hills. Used for roads and settlements, so it matters most early on.

Lumber

Comes from forests. Pairs with brick for roads and settlements.

Grain

Comes from fields. Needed for settlements, cities, and development cards, so it's useful all game long.

Wool

Comes from pastures. Goes into settlements and development cards, and it's a good trade item if you have extra.

Ore

Comes from mountains. The key to upgrading to cities and buying development cards.

Resource tips

  • Try to touch at least three different resource types with your starting settlements.
  • If one resource is rare on the board, claim it early or be ready to trade for it.
  • Brick and lumber drive early expansion; ore and grain drive the late game.
  • Harbors make extra resources more valuable, since they improve your trade rates.
