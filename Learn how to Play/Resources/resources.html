Catan Resources

In Catan, there are 5 main resources used to build roads, settlements, cities, and development cards. Each tile on the board produces a specific resource when its number is rolled.

The 5 Resources

Brick

Produced by Hills tiles. Used for building roads and settlements.

Lumber (Wood)

Produced by Forest tiles. Used for building roads and settlements.

Grain (Wheat)

Produced by Fields tiles. Used for settlements, cities, and development cards.

Wool (Sheep)

Produced by Pasture tiles. Used for settlements and development cards.

Ore

Produced by Mountains tiles. Used for upgrading cities and buying development cards.

Building Costs

Structure / Card Required Resources Victory Points
Road 1 Brick + 1 Lumber 0 (2 pts for Longest Road)
Settlement 1 Brick + 1 Lumber + 1 Grain + 1 Wool 1 VP
City (Upgrade) 3 Ore + 2 Grain 2 VP
Development Card 1 Ore + 1 Grain + 1 Wool Varies (1 VP cards)

Resource Tips

  • Place starting settlements next to tiles with high-probability numbers (6 and 8).
  • Aim for resource diversity early so you aren't stuck waiting on trades.
  • Watch your hand size—rolling a 7 forces anyone with more than 7 cards to discard half!
