Understanding the Board

The Island of Catan

The board is built from 19 terrain hexes surrounded by ocean frame pieces and 9 harbors (ports). Because the hexes and number tokens are shuffled before each game, the island layout changes every time you play.

Board Components

19 Terrain Hexes

4 Forest (Wood), 4 Pasture (Sheep), 4 Fields (Wheat), 3 Hills (Brick), 3 Mountains (Ore), and 1 Desert (produces nothing).

Number Tokens (2–12)

Placed on every terrain hex except the Desert. When two dice match a tile's number, adjacent settlements and cities collect that resource.

Red Numbers (6 & 8)

6 and 8 are printed in red because they are the most frequently rolled totals on two 6-sided dice. Red numbers can never be placed next to each other during setup.

The Robber

Starts on the Desert. Whenever a 7 is rolled, the Robber moves to block a tile from producing resources and lets the active player steal 1 card.

Harbors (Ports)

  • 3:1 Generic Harbors (4 total): Trade any 3 identical resource cards for 1 resource of your choice.
  • 2:1 Specialty Harbors (5 total): Trade 2 of a specific resource (Brick, Wood, Wheat, Sheep, or Ore) for any 1 resource.
